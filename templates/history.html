Asian Parliamentary Debate

Two sides. One motion. A test of reasoning.

Debate League follows the Asian Parliamentary Debate format. One side defends the motion as Government, while the other challenges it as Opposition.

Success depends on clear structure, sharp analysis, strong rebuttals and teamwork under pressure.

Before the Arguments

The League starts before the first motion.

Captains → Player Pool → Auction → Squads → League. The auction adds a tactical edge before the first motion, turning preparation into part of the drama.

Behind the League

Literary and Debating Club, NIT Jalandhar

At LADC, NIT Jalandhar, debate is not treated as an extra activity. It is a culture of reading, argument, rebuttal and public speaking that has grown through student-led participation and repeated competitive seasons.

Debate League is the club’s competitive expression of that culture: a space where beginners and experienced speakers meet the same motion, the same pressure and the same opportunity to improve.

Think. Express. Evolve.

About the Event

The sixth chapter of the LADC Debate League

The LADC Debate League is built around sustained competition rather than a single round. Teams compete through the league stage, build their position in the standings and fight for qualification into the knockouts.

Across the tournament, speakers are challenged to develop stronger arguments, respond under pressure, work effectively with their teammates and improve through repeated competitive debating.

DebLiga serves as the digital home of the tournament — maintaining schedules, results, standings, team records and speaker performances.

Beyond the League

About LADC

The Literary and Debating Club (LADC), NIT Jalandhar is one of the oldest student clubs on campus, with a history stretching back more than two decades.

Its alumni have gone on to careers in public service, policy and leadership, including paths linked to the Indian Foreign Service and other high-profile professional spaces.

The Debate League is one part of that larger culture — giving both newer speakers and experienced debaters a place to learn, compete and grow.
