A concise record of the League champions through each edition. 2026 is the current edition and the champion is to be decided.

The LADC Debate League is a recurring collegiate debating competition focused on argumentation, rebuttal, public speaking and learning through repeated competition. It began in 2021 and has continued annually.

The League emphasises clarity of thought, teamwork and the development of persuasive speaking. This page preserves the champions list and a short timeline of the League's editions.
