Compulsory Participation Rule

Every registered member of a team must participate in at least one debate during the league/group stage.

Teams may repeat speakers across their three league-stage matches. Some speakers may participate in all three matches while other members are rotated across the remaining speaking positions. By the end of the three league-stage debates, every registered member must have participated at least once; equal participation and an unchanged speaker combination are not required.
