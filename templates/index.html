About the Tournament

The sixth chapter of the LADC Debate League

The LADC Debate League is a sustained college debating competition built around preparation, league play and progression—not a single isolated round.

Teams build their squads, compete across the season and fight for a place in the knockouts while every speaker develops through repeated, adjudicated debate.
