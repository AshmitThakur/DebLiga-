About the Event

The sixth chapter of the LADC Debate League

The LADC Debate League is built around sustained competition rather than a single round. Teams compete through the league stage, build their position in the standings and fight for qualification into the knockouts.

Across the tournament, speakers are challenged to develop stronger arguments, respond under pressure, work effectively with their teammates and improve through repeated competitive debating.

DebLiga serves as the digital home of the tournament — maintaining schedules, results, standings, team records and speaker performances.

Beyond the League

About LADC

The Literary and Debating Club (LADC), NIT Jalandhar is a student community centred around debating, public speaking, literary expression and the exchange of ideas.

Through debates, speaking activities, discussions and competitive opportunities, the club provides a space for students to develop confidence, critical thinking, communication and the ability to build and defend ideas.

The Debate League is one part of that larger culture — giving both newer speakers and experienced debaters an environment to learn, compete and improve over multiple rounds.
